For a review that asks you to verify a recommendation, independently test its factual premise and decide whether to accept or reject it; that evidence-based adjudication can warrant Sol at medium effort. Do not equate it with a bounded checklist that merely enumerates known concerns, which remains Luna at high or xhigh effort.

For architecture work that spans system boundaries, data ownership, and rollout strategy, treat the reasoning as exceptionally demanding; choose a stronger eligible model and high or xhigh effort when justified.
